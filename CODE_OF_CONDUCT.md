# Code of conduct

Be decent. Concretely:

- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements here are about trade-offs, not
  character. Argue with the design, never the person.
- **Take the subject matter seriously.** This project handles people's private
  thoughts. Don't post someone else's persona data, don't test against other
  people's accounts, and don't treat a privacy bug as a joke.
- **Harassment, slurs, and personal attacks** get your comment removed and,
  if repeated, get you blocked. That includes doing it "ironically".
- **Feedback is a gift, bluntness is fine, contempt isn't.** "This breaks
  tenant isolation, here's how" is welcome. "This is garbage" isn't.

Report anything that needs attention privately via
[a security advisory](https://github.com/bishesh910/opersona/security/advisories/new)
or by emailing the maintainer. Reports are handled discreetly.

The maintainer has the final say on what stays and who participates.
